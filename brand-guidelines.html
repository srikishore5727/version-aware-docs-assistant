VADA is an internal developer tool that answers questions from product documentation and always shows the document and version behind the answer. The brand should feel like the product: calm, exact, and impossible to misread.

01

Foundation

What the brand stands for, before any visual decision is made.

Positioning

A documentation answer engine for developers. It retrieves the exact passage and cites it. It does not speculate.

Promise

Every answer can be verified in one click. Trust comes from traceability, not from confidence of tone.

Personality

Precise

Version numbers, field names, exact errors. Never approximate.

Calm

No urgency, no exclamation marks, no celebration of routine actions.

Honest

Says "not found" without apology or padding.

Quietly technical

Speaks the reader's language. No marketing voice inside the product.

Design principle: when in doubt, remove. The interface should look like a tool, not a landing page.

Logo

A geometric V with a single dot beneath it. The V is the name; the dot is the citation mark — every answer ends with its source.

Clear space

Clear space on all sides equals x, where x is the diameter of the dot × 4. Nothing — text, borders, or other logos — enters this zone.

Usage

Do

Use the primary pair on light surfaces. Keep the dot indigo; keep the V ink.

Do

On dark surfaces, switch to the inverse mark and allow the dot to brighten.

Don't

Never stretch, squash, rotate, or outline the mark.

Don't

Do not recolour the mark outside the approved palette.

Rules

  • 70 / 20 / 10. Roughly 70% neutral surface, 20% ink text, 10% accent. If indigo covers more than a tenth of a screen, remove some.
  • One accent. Indigo means "interactive" or "cited". It never decorates a heading.
  • Amber is a warning only. It appears for the not-found state and data-quality notices, nowhere else.
  • Never rely on colour alone. Pair every state with a label or icon.

Rules

  • One typeface. Weight and spacing create hierarchy, not a second font.
  • Headings tighten, body loosens. Negative tracking above 22 px, normal tracking at body size.
  • Code is always monospace and never uses the accent colour.
  • Line length stays under 70 characters for prose.

Motion explains change; it never entertains. Nothing bounces, nothing overshoots.

Easing: cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .3, 1). Respect prefers-reduced-motion and disable animation entirely when set.

08

Accessibility

Requirements, not aspirations. Every screen is reviewed against this list.

  • Contrast: body text at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1. The muted grey on paper passes at 4.6:1.
  • Focus: a 2 px indigo ring with 2 px offset on every interactive element. Never remove it.
  • Targets: 44 × 44 px minimum on touch.
  • Announcements: answers and errors use aria-live="polite".
  • Colour independence: every status has text or an icon beside it.
  • Keyboard: every flow completes without a mouse.
